We are advertising every new car for more
than the price we actually sell it for.

Autotrader and cars.com · Every price below was read off the live sites on 27 July 2026

Our website is correct. The feed we send to the marketplaces is not. It sends each car's price before the factory customer bonus is applied. So the shopper on Autotrader or cars.com sees a higher number than the one we will actually take — and a higher number than our own website shows them.

Our two deepest-stocked models — 34 cars. A shopper filtering by price never reaches us. This is consistent with the share we have lost since the pricing tool changed: the previous tool published the after-rebate price; the current feed does not.

What we are not asking for

  • Not a price cut. Not one dollar of gross changes.
  • Not our money. The bonus is factory cash, already funded.
  • Not a new offer. It is the same price we already advertise on our own website and already honor in the store.

We are asking to publish the price we are already selling at.

The fix

One field in each feed. Not a project.

  1. cars.com — send the after-bonus price, and move the $698 dealer fee out of the price field.
  2. Autotrader — send the after-bonus price, keep the fee in the price, and take it back out of MSRP.

The two sites quote fees differently, so one global change fixes one and breaks the other. They must be set separately.
